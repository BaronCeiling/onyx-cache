# Release notes

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

First release. What is in it:

* Breadcrumbs shortcode and Website/Article JSON-LD
* Locally cached score with recommendations
* Backup of the redirect table and settings
* Spam and trash sweep on a configurable schedule
* WebP and AVIF copies generated on upload
* Async image decoding with fetch priority on the hero image
* REST purge route behind capability checks
* 404 log with hit counts and one-click 301 creation

Nothing was removed or deprecated, because nothing existed before this tag.
